Hi, I'm Maria-Teresa! :)

I'm a third-year PhD researcher at the Digital Society Initiative at the University of Zurich, advised by Dr. Eva Cetinić. My PhD is part of the SNSF project The Canon of Latent Spaces: How Large AI Models Encode Art and Culture.

My research focuses on multimodal generative AI, with an emphasis on how models encode cultural and societal concepts. I study questions around cultural knowledge, human values, representation, and bias, and develop evaluation methods to uncover the defaults these systems reproduce. Ultimately, I am interested in how these insights can help us build AI systems that are more fair, inclusive, and aligned with diverse human perspectives, while preserving rather than flattening the complexity of cultural and social realities.

During my PhD, I've also had the opportunity to work across academia and industry, most recently as a research intern at the Sony AI Ethics team.

If you'd like to chat about multimodal AI, culture, evaluation, or research more generally, feel free to reach out!

Interests  Generative AI · Multimodal Models · Fairness & Bias · Cultural & Societal AI · Model Evaluation & Explainability
